How this works

1. All 39 users are shown with their original AI Decision Matrix.

2. Edit Your Decision or Account Type dropdowns — changed rows glow amber.

3. Click ➕ Add User to add a new seat with full attributes — new rows glow teal.

4. Click 🔄 Regenerate from AI Baseline to re-derive the recommended decisions from scratch based on each user's survey/activity data.

5. Click ✨ Analyse My Scenario to get a full AI assessment comparing your decisions against the original Decision Matrix.

6. 💾 Save Template to store your scenario and load it anytime.
